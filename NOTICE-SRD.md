The PDF is not in this repository. Download it free from Wizards of the Coast at
https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/srd/5.2/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf (file `SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf`) and open it in the
app, or save it as `books/srd-5.2.1.pdf` for development; the app shows its pages from your own copy.

What is here: names, numbers and tables (classes, species, backgrounds, feats, spells, equipment, conditions, XP),
monsters (all 331 stat blocks: size, type, alignment, Armor Class, Initiative, Hit Points and their dice, speeds,
abilities and saves, skills, resistances and immunities, senses, languages, challenge, XP, proficiency, and each
trait's and action's name with its numbers: attack bonus, reach or range, damage dice and type, save and DC,
recharge, uses), magic items (all 258: kind, rarity, attunement, charges, bonuses, damage dice, the spells they cast),
page regions, and one-line summaries in our own words. The sample players, adventurers, sessions, notes and threads
are original.
