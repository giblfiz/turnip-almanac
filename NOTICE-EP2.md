The PDF is not in this repository. The book is free to download and share; get it from the co-author's share linked
from https://robboyle.info/ (file `EclipsePhase_SecondEdition_v1.3.pdf`) and open it in the app, or save it as
`books/EclipsePhase_SecondEdition_v1.3.pdf` for development. The app recognises that exact file by its fingerprint
and shows its pages, and cuts its illustrations, from your own copy, in your browser.

What is here: the Almanac's Eclipse Phase stencil holds page locations (page numbers and rectangles), names
(aptitudes, skills, packages, morphs, traits, ware, gear, sleights, rep networks), mechanics arranged in our own
structure, and our own one-line rule summaries. It reproduces no setting text, fiction or art: setting, fiction and
history chapters carry labels only, and every picture is cut at runtime from the reader's own copy. The sample
team (players Sam, Riley, Ines and Theo), their agents, sessions, people, places and threads are original and use no
characters or plot from the book.

If richer summaries are ever added, they would be published under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 like the rest of these files.
