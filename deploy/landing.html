Your own copy of the book

Each table runs on a rulebook you bring. The pages, and any art from the book, are drawn from your own copy, in your browser; the book never touches our server.

  1. On the table, follow the link to download the book from its maker.
  2. Open that file on the table (or drop it on the page). The table checks that it is the exact file it was made for.
  3. It stays in this browser, so next time it is already there.

What is saved

Your table (characters, notes, rolls and every change) is kept in this browser. There are no accounts and nothing is shared yet.
